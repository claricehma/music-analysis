Every composer has different styles, patterns, and other unique features that make their music stand apart. For example, Mozart is known for graceful melodies, whereas Bach tends to prefer scale patterns. In this research project, we aim to train various types of models to predict the composer of the pieces given. 

Our data from Kaggle MusicNet Dataset consists of around 320 compositions by ten composers (Bach, Beethoven, Brahms, Cambini, Dvorak, Faure, Haydn, Mozart, Ravel, Schubert). Using three types of supervised machine learning models, we will analyze music pieces part of our training set to predict the composers of ten pieces. Our three models are Neural Network, K-Nearest Neighbors, and XGBoost package. 

At the end, we will compare each model’s accuracy and efficiency when faced with new, unseen data.

Kaggle MusicNet Dataset link here: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/imsparsh/musicnet-dataset/data
